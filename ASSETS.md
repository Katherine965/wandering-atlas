## Excluded from first release

Stranger Things, Rusty Lake and other non-owned film/game themes are not release candidates. Their asset directories are absent and they are not registered themes. Historical excluded manifest entries do not license them. The release check rejects reintroduced asset directories under public/travel/themes outside the five included themes.

Personal archives, .local/, public/uploads/, backups, node_modules and test artifacts are excluded from source releases. An explicit personal archive export requires separate privacy and photo-rights review. Original sample SVGs are not travel photographs; user uploads receive no automatic project license.
